Journey Highlights

Selected work & moments

Sangyaw Festival 2026

Tacloban, Philippines June 2026

Returning the following year, I served as a Videographer and Live Stream Operator throughout Tacloban City’s month-long celebration, continuing to document one of the region’s most celebrated cultural events.

Capturing the festival from behind the camera and helping bring it to audiences in real time made this experience especially meaningful. It was a privilege to help tell the story of my hometown through every moment I captured.

This wasn’t just another production. It was a celebration I was proud to help tell.

Enigma Music Fest 2026

Tacloban, Philippines June 2026

Representing Blaze Kultura as a Photographer, I had the privilege of documenting Enigma Music Fest 2026, capturing the excitement of one of the region’s biggest live music events.

Experiences like this are rare in our region, making every moment behind the camera even more meaningful. It’s an opportunity I’m truly grateful to have been part of.

Some moments are captured through a lens—others stay with you long after the music ends.

Project for Aerial Recovery

Cebu, Philippines October 2025

When the 6.9 earthquake struck Central Cebu, I spent a week on the ground documenting the response alongside Aerial Recovery and Global Empowerment Mission.

This was a volunteer effort—an opportunity I chose to be part of. Moving through affected communities, I focused on the urgency, quiet strength, and human side of every moment.

More than documenting the mission, this film reflects the people, emotions, and resilience I witnessed firsthand.

Sangyaw Festival 2025

Tacloban, Philippines June 2025

Throughout Tacloban City’s month-long celebration, I had the opportunity to serve as a Videographer, Video Editor, and Live Stream Operator, helping document and share one of the region’s most celebrated cultural events.

From filming and editing to supporting the live broadcast, I contributed to preserving the stories, traditions, and moments that brought the celebration to life.

This experience laid the foundation for the role I would take on the following year.
